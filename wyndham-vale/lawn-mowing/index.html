Service in Wyndham Vale

Practical care for separate lawns and compact yards.

Even a small lawn can involve several separate areas and plenty of edging. Include the front lawn, rear yard and verge when describing the job, along with side-gate access and any garden borders you want kept neat.

Small and medium lawn areas

Mowing can include separate front and rear lawns as well as the nature strip. Show each area in the photos so the quote reflects the complete job. Edge work along paths, driveways and garden beds can be discussed alongside the mowing.

A manageable maintenance routine

If you want the lawn kept tidy throughout the season, ask about repeat visits. Share whether the grass is already maintained or needs an initial cut-back. The proposed routine should suit its growth and your property rather than assuming every lawn needs the same schedule.

Move-in and rental-yard tidy-ups

A one-off visit can help when taking over a yard or getting ready for a property inspection. Mention your preferred date and show any long grass, hidden obstacles or piles of garden waste. Additional garden or rubbish work should be included in the enquiry so it can be quoted.

Questions

Lawn Mowing questions.

Can you quote for a small backyard?

Yes. Send photos of the lawn and access, even for a compact yard. Separate lawn sections, edging and the route to the backyard all affect the work involved.

What if the backyard has a narrow side gate?

Mention the gate width and any steps when enquiring. Photos of the route help Jack assess equipment access before confirming the job.

Can the front lawn and nature strip be done together?

Yes. Show both in your enquiry and identify any additional frontage you want maintained so the quote includes it.

Do I need ongoing service to book a tidy-up?

No. You can request a single visit. If the lawn is overgrown, send recent photos so Jack can allow for the extra work.
