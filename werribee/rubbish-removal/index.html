Service in Werribee

Household and outdoor collections with a clear scope.

A property clear-out may involve items in several places: inside the home, along a side path or in a backyard shed. Identify all collection areas at the start so the quote accounts for the full load, lifting and access.

Household and garage clear-outs

Clear agreed rubbish and unwanted items to reclaim storage space or prepare for a move. Photograph each area separately and distinguish items that are staying. Let Jack know about access arrangements for a rental property or unit before setting a collection date.

Bulky furniture and appliances

For furniture and appliance removal, include item dimensions and the route out of the building. Stairs, doorways, shared access and lifting requirements can affect the quote. Jack will confirm whether the items and disposal needs suit the collection.

Green waste and outdoor debris

Garden clean-ups can leave branches, clippings, timber and mixed rubbish that need different handling. Show each material clearly rather than photographing only the top of a pile. Describe any related garden work if you would like it assessed alongside removal.

Questions

Rubbish Removal questions.

Can you quote for rubbish in several parts of a property?

Yes. Provide a separate photo of each pile and explain where it is located. This helps Jack assess the whole job rather than only the most visible load.

What information is needed for a unit or rental property?

Include the items, access route and any stairs or shared access restrictions. Explain how the visit will be arranged and provide a handover date if relevant.

Can old furniture be included with garden waste?

Ask for both to be quoted and show them separately. The type of material and its disposal requirements can affect which items are accepted and the total price.

Is rubbish collection an alternative to a skip?

For an agreed load, Reggie’s can collect the items without you arranging a skip. Photos help establish whether the service fits your clear-out and what it will cost.
