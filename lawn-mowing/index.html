What we do

Lawn Mowing for homes, rentals and outdoor areas.

Based in Eynesbury and servicing Melton, Wyndham Vale, Werribee, Caroline Springs, Manor Lakes and nearby suburbs within approximately 25 km.

Regular lawn mowing and edging

Arrange repeat mowing for front lawns, backyards and nature strips, with edging around paths, driveways and garden borders. Discuss visit frequency and whether clippings removal should be included in your quote.

One-off and overgrown lawn mowing

Bring long grass and untidy edges back under control before a rental inspection, move or seasonal tidy-up. Send current photos of all grassed areas, grass height and access so Jack can assess the initial work separately from ongoing maintenance.

Acreage mowing and slashing

For larger blocks, send an approximate area and photos showing slopes, obstacles, grass height and vehicle access. Jack can assess whether acreage mowing or slashing suits the property and discuss any green waste removal.
