USEFUL ANSWERS

Your questions, covered.

Can you assess an overgrown block?

Yes. Send wide photos, the approximate area and details of grass height, access, slopes and obstacles. Jack will confirm whether the work is suitable and what is included before a booking is agreed.

Do I need to know whether the job needs mowing or slashing?

No. Describe the area and the result you want, and include current photos. Jack can assess the job and discuss an appropriate approach rather than asking you to choose the method in advance.

What affects an acreage mowing quote?

The area, grass condition, terrain, obstacles, vehicle access and agreed scope all help determine the work involved. Mention separate sections and anything that could be hidden in long grass.

Can I ask about ongoing maintenance?

Yes. Include that in your enquiry and explain how the area is currently maintained. Jack can discuss repeat work after reviewing the property and the initial job.

Can you remove rubbish or green waste too?

Ask for it to be assessed with the mowing. Photograph existing rubbish and green-waste piles separately and identify what needs to go. Collection and any disposal requirements must be included in the agreed quote.
