Yes. Provide a separate photo of each pile and explain where it is located. This helps Jack assess the whole job rather than only the most visible load.

What information is needed for a unit or rental property?

Can old furniture be included with garden waste?

Ask for both to be quoted and show them separately. The type of material and its disposal requirements can affect which items are accepted and the total price.
