Service in Werribee

Lawn maintenance with the whole yard in view.

Established garden borders, trees and separate lawn areas can make a yard more involved than its size suggests. Photos of each section help Jack assess the mowing and trimming required, along with access and any longer grass.

Mowing around established garden areas

Show the lawn alongside beds, trees, paths and fences so the work can be assessed accurately. Mowing and defined edges can help keep grassed areas tidy without overlooking the smaller sections. Mention exposed irrigation, ornaments or other features that need care.

Repeat care for homes and rentals

Discuss regular visits if you want ongoing upkeep for your own home or a rental property. Set out which areas are included and how access will be arranged. The mowing interval can be considered against the grass growth and seasonal conditions.

Long grass and property handovers

If the lawn has been left for a while, send recent photos rather than estimating from a previous visit. Grass height, obstacles and garden encroachment can add work. Include the date of any inspection, sale preparation or move so availability can be confirmed.

Questions

Lawn Mowing questions.

Can you maintain a rental property’s lawns?

Yes. Provide the lawn areas, access arrangements and whether you need a one-off visit or regular mowing. Include any inspection or handover date in the enquiry.

What should I show in photos of an established yard?

Show the full lawn plus trees, garden beds, narrow sections and access gates. Mention any hidden items or irrigation features that may not be obvious in the photos.

Do you include lawn edging?

Mowing and edge work can be quoted together. Show the borders along paths, driveways and garden beds, especially if grass has grown over them.

Can you help with garden waste after a tidy-up?

Ask Jack to include removal in the quote. Separate photos of branches, clippings or existing piles help establish how much needs to be collected.
