Care for compact lawns and landscaped spaces.

When lawns sit between paths, garden beds and courtyard features, access and edge work matter as much as the area of grass. Describe each section to be maintained and show the route through side gates or shared access.

Courtyard and townhouse lawns

Small or separated lawns can be assessed alongside larger front yards. Show the access route, steps and available gate width in your enquiry. These details help Jack confirm whether equipment can reach each area and what work is practical.

Edges around paths and garden beds

Include photos of lawn borders, driveway edges and narrow strips of grass. Edging can be quoted with mowing to give these areas a tidy finish. Point out irrigation, low garden features or other obstacles that may be difficult to see.

Questions

Lawn Mowing questions.

Can you mow a townhouse lawn or courtyard?

Ask with photos of the grassed area and its access. Jack will assess the gate width, steps and equipment access before confirming the work.

Can edging be included on a small lawn?

Yes. Show paths, garden borders and narrow grass strips in your enquiry. A small lawn can still involve substantial edge work, which should be included in the quote.

What if access is shared?

Explain how access works and any restrictions when requesting the quote. Confirm the arrangements for the visit with Jack before the booking.

Can I request a single visit before an inspection?

Yes. Send the required date and current photos. Jack will confirm availability and whether the lawn needs an initial tidy-up beyond routine mowing.
