A useful quote starts with the whole property, not just the front lawn. Include the backyard, nature strip and any separate grassed sections. For larger blocks or long grass, show the overall area so Jack can assess whether mowing or slashing is the better fit.

Residential lawns and defined edges

Mowing and edge work can cover front lawns, backyards, paths and garden borders. Identify small lawn sections and areas around fences that are easy to miss in a wide photo. Discuss clippings removal when requesting the quote so the finish matches what you need.

Seasonal, ongoing maintenance

Regular mowing can be arranged around the way your lawn grows through the season. Tell Jack about your current mowing routine and any areas that become difficult to manage between visits. The frequency and scope can then be discussed for your property.

Larger yards and overgrown areas

For an extended yard, block edge or overgrown section, send wide photos and an estimate of the area. Show slopes, obstacles and access points as well as grass height. Reggie’s also offers acreage mowing and slashing, which can be assessed where standard lawn mowing is not suitable.
