Questions

Lawn Mowing questions.

Can you maintain a rental property’s lawns?

Yes. Provide the lawn areas, access arrangements and whether you need a one-off visit or regular mowing. Include any inspection or handover date in the enquiry.

What should I show in photos of an established yard?

Show the full lawn plus trees, garden beds, narrow sections and access gates. Mention any hidden items or irrigation features that may not be obvious in the photos.

Do you include lawn edging?

Mowing and edge work can be quoted together. Show the borders along paths, driveways and garden beds, especially if grass has grown over them.

Can you help with garden waste after a tidy-up?

Ask Jack to include removal in the quote. Separate photos of branches, clippings or existing piles help establish how much needs to be collected.
