Questions

Lawn Mowing questions.

Can you quote for a small backyard?

Yes. Send photos of the lawn and access, even for a compact yard. Separate lawn sections, edging and the route to the backyard all affect the work involved.

What if the backyard has a narrow side gate?

Mention the gate width and any steps when enquiring. Photos of the route help Jack assess equipment access before confirming the job.

Can the front lawn and nature strip be done together?

Yes. Show both in your enquiry and identify any additional frontage you want maintained so the quote includes it.

Do I need ongoing service to book a tidy-up?

No. You can request a single visit. If the lawn is overgrown, send recent photos so Jack can allow for the extra work.
