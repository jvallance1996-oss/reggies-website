What we do

Rubbish Removal for homes, rentals and outdoor areas.

Based in Eynesbury, we offer rubbish removal in Melton, Wyndham Vale, Werribee, Caroline Springs, Manor Lakes and nearby suburbs within approximately 25 km.

House and garage clean-outs

Clear unwanted household items, packaging and accumulated clutter from garages, sheds and storage areas. Let Jack know what needs to go, what should stay and whether you have a moving or handover date. Photos of the full load and access help define the job.

Furniture, appliance and general rubbish removal

Ask about collecting unwanted furniture, appliances and mixed household rubbish. Include approximate sizes and mention stairs, narrow doors or difficult lifting. Jack will confirm the agreed items and disposal requirements before booking.

Green waste and garden debris removal

Remove branches, clippings and garden debris after pruning, mowing or a yard tidy-up. Include photos of any timber or mixed materials so they can be assessed separately when quoting. You can also ask about combining collection with garden maintenance.
