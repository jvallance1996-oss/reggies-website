    BEFORE WE GET STARTED

    A few useful answers.

    Can I request a quote without calling?

    Yes. Use the quote form and choose a text or email reply, or text job photos directly to Jack on 0437 844 844.

    Do you offer regular and one-off work?

    Yes. Ask about a one-off job or regular maintenance. An overgrown first visit can involve different work from ongoing upkeep, so the scope and quote are agreed with you.

    What do you need for a quote?

    Start with your suburb, service and contact details. Photos of the whole area or rubbish load, plus access and any timing requirements, help Jack assess the work.

    Can you quote for more than one job?

    Yes. Mention the other tasks in your enquiry and photograph each area separately so Jack can assess the full job.

    Is sending a request a confirmed booking?

    No. Jack confirms the work, price and availability with you before a booking is agreed.
