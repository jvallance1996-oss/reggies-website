USEFUL ANSWERS

Your questions, covered.

Can I book a one-off garden tidy-up?

Yes. Describe the beds, hedges and other areas that need attention, and include any required date. Jack will confirm the scope and availability before a visit is agreed.

Can gardening and lawn mowing be quoted together?

Yes. Include photos of the lawn as well as the garden and identify all the tasks you want assessed. The quote can then reflect the agreed work across the property.

Is green-waste removal included?

Ask for it to be included when requesting your quote. Show existing piles separately from the areas to be maintained, and identify any timber or mixed rubbish so the collection can be assessed.

Can I arrange regular garden maintenance?

Yes. Tell Jack which tasks you want maintained and how the garden is currently managed. Discuss the initial tidy-up and ongoing work so each visit has a clear scope.

What photos help with a gardening quote?

Take a wide view of each area, then closer photos of the work you want done. Show the hedge height, side access, obstacles and any waste to remove. Point out plants or sections that should stay untouched.
