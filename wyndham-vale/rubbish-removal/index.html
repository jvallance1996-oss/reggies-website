Make space after a move or household clear-out.

Boxes and packaging can take over usable space quickly, while bulky items need a little more planning. Send a view of the complete load rather than just one section, and include your preferred timing if the collection is part of a move or property handover.

Packaging and household clutter

Ask about removing accumulated boxes, packaging and agreed household rubbish from a garage or storage area. Show loose items as well as bagged waste. If there are several separate piles, photograph each one so the quoted volume reflects the whole job.

Furniture and appliance collection

List the bulky items you want removed and provide approximate dimensions where useful. Mention narrow side access, stairs or items stored behind other furniture. Jack will assess lifting, access and disposal requirements before confirming what can be collected.

Yard tidy-up and green waste loads

Branches, clippings and garden debris can be included in a rubbish-removal enquiry. Keep photos of green waste distinct from packaging or other mixed rubbish. If mowing or garden maintenance is also needed, describe that work when requesting the quote.

Questions

Rubbish Removal questions.

Can you remove packaging after a move?

Ask with photos showing all the boxes, packaging and other items. Identify the materials and any bulky objects so Jack can confirm the collection scope.

Can I add furniture to a garage clear-out?

Yes, ask for it to be assessed in the same enquiry. Show each piece and explain its location, size and access requirements rather than assuming it is covered by a photo of the smaller rubbish.

Can collection be arranged before a handover?

Provide the date as early as possible with current photos. Jack will confirm whether the job can be scheduled; a requested date is not a booking until agreed.

What if there is limited side access?

Send a photo of the access route and mention narrow gates, steps or other obstructions. These details help establish the lifting and time needed.
