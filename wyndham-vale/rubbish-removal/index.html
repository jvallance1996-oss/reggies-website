Questions

Rubbish Removal questions.

Can you remove packaging after a move?

Ask with photos showing all the boxes, packaging and other items. Identify the materials and any bulky objects so Jack can confirm the collection scope.

Can I add furniture to a garage clear-out?

Yes, ask for it to be assessed in the same enquiry. Show each piece and explain its location, size and access requirements rather than assuming it is covered by a photo of the smaller rubbish.

Can collection be arranged before a handover?

Provide the date as early as possible with current photos. Jack will confirm whether the job can be scheduled; a requested date is not a booking until agreed.

What if there is limited side access?

Send a photo of the access route and mention narrow gates, steps or other obstructions. These details help establish the lifting and time needed.
