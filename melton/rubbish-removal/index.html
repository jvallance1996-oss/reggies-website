What rubbish can Reggie’s remove?

Reggie’s can quote for household rubbish, furniture, appliances, green waste, branches and outdoor clean-up debris. List the items and identify any unusual materials in your photos so Jack can confirm what can be collected.

Do I need to hire a skip?

No. Reggie’s collects the agreed items, which can be easier for smaller clean-outs and loads where you do not need a skip sitting at the property.

Can you quote from photos?

Yes. Send clear photos showing the whole load, bulky items and the route out of the property. Include your suburb and mention stairs, narrow gates or limited parking. Jack will confirm any details needed to quote and arrange collection.

How much does rubbish removal cost?

Each job is quoted individually. The amount and type of rubbish, lifting, access, travel and disposal fees affect the price. Photos and a description of the load help Jack provide a useful estimate before you book.

Do the items need to be on the nature strip?

Tell Jack where the items are now, whether that is a garage, shed, yard or inside the property. Access and lifting requirements are considered in the quote. Confirm the collection arrangements before moving heavy items.

Can you help with a rental or moving clean-out?

Yes. Ask about removing agreed household items, garage clutter or garden waste before a move or property handover. Include the required date and photos so Jack can confirm the scope and availability.
